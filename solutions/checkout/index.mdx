Checkout Cart lets you collect payments by sharing a simple payment link wherever your customers already spend time. You do not need to build a product catalog or storefront. Customers open the link, complete payment, and you track received and outstanding amounts in real time.

**Use it for:** Collecting payments from conversations, social posts, websites, and any channel where a direct link is the fastest way to get paid.

## How it works

1. You create a payment link for the amount you want to collect.
2. You share the link through chat, a social post, or on your website.
3. A customer opens the link and completes the payment.
4. You track received and outstanding amounts in real time.

## Minimum preparation

Before you start collecting payments, prepare the following:

- A payment amount and description for what you are collecting.
- A channel for sharing the link with customers, such as chat, social media, or a website.

For official account requirements and eligibility, contact [hello@feezless.cloud](mailto:hello@feezless.cloud).

## Pricing

Contact Feezless for the current transaction rate and applicable charges before accepting payments.

As an illustration only, if the transaction rate were 2.0%, a 1,000 currency unit payment would incur a 20 currency unit fee. This is an example, not an approved rate.

> **Note:** Creating a payment link or receiving a transfer proof alone does not confirm payment. Check the confirmed payment status before you deliver goods or services.
