Product Menu turns your catalog into a single-page storefront where customers browse items, add them to a cart, and pay without leaving the page. It is built for restaurants, cafes, retail shops, and any business that wants an online menu with built-in checkout.

## Use it for

- Restaurant and cafe menus with online ordering
- Retail product catalogs with cart and checkout
- Any business that wants browsing, cart, and payment on one page

## How it works

1. Build your product or menu catalog with names, descriptions, and prices.
2. Customers browse the menu, add items to their cart, and proceed to checkout on the same page.
3. Inventory is deducted automatically when a sale is completed.

> **Note:** Creating a payment link or completing checkout alone does not confirm payment. Check confirmed payment status before fulfilling the order.

## Minimum preparation

Before you start, prepare the following:

- Product or menu item names and prices
- Opening quantities for each item if you want to track stock
- A clear understanding of which items you will offer online
