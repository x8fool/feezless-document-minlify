With Feezless Invoice and bill payment, create an invoice and send your customer an online payment link. Track which invoices are paid and which remain outstanding in real time, without preparing a separate billing document manually.

## Use case

Use this solution when you want to:

- Bill customers for services or orders.
- Give customers a link to pay online.
- Track paid bills and identify those that need follow-up.

## Minimum preparation

Before creating an invoice, prepare the following:

- **Billing details:** what the customer is being charged for.
- **Amount to collect:** the amount due from the customer.
- **Contact channel:** how you will send the payment link to the customer.

For account requirements and setup, contact [hello@feezless.cloud](mailto:hello@feezless.cloud).

## How it works

1. You create an invoice with the billing details and amount.
2. Feezless generates an online payment link for that invoice.
3. You send the link to the customer through your preferred contact channel.
4. The customer opens the link and pays online.
5. You track the invoice status in real time to see whether it is paid or outstanding.

> **Note:** Sending a payment link alone does not confirm payment. Check the confirmed payment status before delivering goods or services.
