Feezless's compliance and trust standards cover entity registration, protection of personal information, transparent commercial terms, and separation of internal access from client-facing operations.

## Entity verification status

Feezless is officially registered with the Department of Business Development (DBD), Ministry of Commerce, Thailand.

- **Registered legal name:** FEEZLESS REGISTERED ORDINARY PARTNERSHIP
- **Registration number:** 0432569000015
- **Country:** Thailand

## Data privacy and protection (PDPA)

Feezless adheres to Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) guidelines. Personally identifiable information (PII) and identity numbers are masked on public-facing registration documents.

See the [Privacy Policy](/th/legal/privacy-policy) for information about personal data processing and privacy requests.

## Operational and commercial transparency

All payment gateway fees, escrow or hold periods, and middleman trading rates are clearly communicated through official service schedules.

See the [Terms of Use](/th/legal/terms-of-use) for service terms, fees, refunds, and dispute handling.

## Credentials and security isolation

System credentials, administrative access, and internal quality assurance (QA) testing procedures are strictly isolated from client-facing operations.

## Trust and compliance contact

For trust and compliance inquiries, contact [security@feezless.cloud](mailto:security@feezless.cloud).
