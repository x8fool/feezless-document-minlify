## True Pay-Per-Request Protocol

**Use it for:** Paid API requests and access to digital services.

Make individual API requests payable instead of requiring a subscription. Unlock the digital response or service when the payment conditions are met.

- Charge by request or use.
- Connect the request, payment, and digital delivery in one flow.
- Include pricing, the payment recipient, and terms of service in the protocol.

## Payment Link

**Use it for:** Collecting payments from conversations, social posts, and websites.

Create a payment link and share it where your customers already are. Customers open the link to pay, and you track the payment status.

- Share links in chat, on social media, or on a website.
- Collect payments without building a product catalog.
- See received and outstanding payments in real time.

## Product Menu

**Use it for:** Product catalogs, restaurant menus, cafes, and retail storefronts.

Bring your products into a single-page storefront. Customers browse the menu, add items to their cart, and complete payment without switching pages.

- Display products in an online menu.
- Keep browsing, cart, and checkout together.
- Deduct stock automatically when a sale is completed.

## POS Cashier

**Use it for:** In-person sales from a phone or tablet.

Use a mobile device as your cashier interface without buying a dedicated checkout device. Record sales and update inventory as you sell.

- Take orders and payments from a phone or tablet.
- Support customers' payment choices at checkout.
- Deduct inventory automatically with each sale.

## Membership and loyalty

**Use it for:** Enrolling customers in your membership program when they pay.

Issue a membership card from the customer's first payment and deliver it through their selected channel. Customers complete their own PIN setup and PDPA consent through a link.

- Create membership cards as part of the payment flow.
- Send the digital card to the customer.
- Let customers set their PIN and provide their own consent.

## Invoice and bill payment

**Use it for:** Billing customers and following up on outstanding payments.

Create an invoice and send a payment link to your customer. Customers pay online while you track which invoices are paid and which remain outstanding.

- Create and send bills without preparing a separate document manually.
- Give customers a link to pay online.
- Monitor payment status in real time.
