## 원클릭 Prompt

전체 블록을 복사해 ChatGPT에 붙여넣고 Feezless 로고 파일을 함께 첨부하세요. 텍스트가 들어간 이미지와 캡션을 한 번에 받을 수 있습니다.

```text Prompt 1 คลิก · Day 05
Create one social media post image for the Feezless brand campaign "10 Days, 10 Different Stories" (Day 05 · Product Menu). Goal: introduce the Product Menu feature.

FORMAT: 4:5 portrait, 1080x1350 px, for Facebook and Instagram feed.

SCENE: E-commerce editorial layout. A neat grid of floating white rounded product cards, each holding a different beautifully lit product (sneaker, coffee bag, skincare bottle, headphones, plant), arranged with precise spacing and soft shadows on a light white-to-pale-blue background, one card subtly highlighted with a blue glow edge. Clean, organized, premium.

HEADLINE: Render this text exactly, spelled correctly, nothing else: "Every Product Has a Place."
Typography: bold modern geometric sans-serif, deep black #0E0E0E, placed in the upper third above the card grid.

LOGO: If I attached the Feezless logo, place it small in the top-left corner exactly as provided. Do not redraw, recolor or alter it. If no logo is attached, leave the corner empty. Never invent a logo.

BRAND STYLE: Premium fintech campaign. Colors: clean white #FDFDFD, deep black #0E0E0E, electric blue gradient accent (cyan to royal blue). Soft studio lighting, high-end editorial quality.

DO NOT: add any other text, prices, product names, people, globes, brand logos on products, watermarks.

After the image, reply with this caption exactly as written, ready to post:

เมื่อมีสินค้าให้เลือกหลายรายการ การนำเสนออย่างเป็นระเบียบช่วยให้ลูกค้ามองเห็นตัวเลือกได้ชัดเจนขึ้น

Product Menu ของ Feezless เป็นฟีเจอร์สำหรับนำเสนอรายการสินค้าในรูปแบบดิจิทัล

อีกหนึ่งเครื่องมือสำหรับธุรกิจที่ต้องการจัดแสดงสิ่งที่ขายให้ลูกค้าเห็นได้อย่างเป็นระบบ

ติดต่อ Feezless เพื่อสอบถามรายละเอียดและสถานะการเปิดใช้งาน Product Menu

#Feezless #ProductMenu #Ecommerce
```

## 1. 이미지

**이미지 문구:** Every Product Has a Place.

**이미지 방향:** Product Cards를 주인공으로 삼아 E-commerce Editorial 작업처럼 구성합니다. 전날처럼 인물이나 지구 이미지는 사용하지 않습니다

```text Prompt ภาพ
E-commerce editorial layout: a neat grid of floating white rounded product cards, each holding a different beautifully lit product (sneaker, coffee bag, skincare bottle, headphones, plant), arranged with precise spacing and soft shadows on a light white-to-pale-blue background, one card subtly highlighted with a blue glow edge. Clean, organized, premium. No people, no globe, no readable text or prices. Premium fintech brand campaign visual with an electric blue gradient accent (cyan to royal blue). Leave empty space in the top-left corner for a logo and space for a headline. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```
