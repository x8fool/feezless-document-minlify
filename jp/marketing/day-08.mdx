## 1. 画像

**画像上のテキスト：** Choose with Clarity.

**画像の方向性：** Corporate Editorial 風の Checklist。明確さと検証しやすさを重視し、認証マークや公式な認定を連想させるシンボルは避ける

```text Prompt ภาพ
Corporate editorial still life: a clean clipboard or tablet with an abstract checklist of four blank lines and blue check marks, placed on a minimal white desk with a pen and soft daylight shadows, calm and precise. Trustworthy, clear, professional. No certification badges, no seals, no shields, no official stamps, no readable text. Premium fintech brand campaign visual, color palette deep black #0E0E0E, clean white #FDFDFD and an electric blue gradient accent (cyan to royal blue). Leave empty space in the top-left corner for a logo and space for a headline. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```
