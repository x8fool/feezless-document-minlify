```text Prompt 1 คลิก · Day 02

SCENE: Moody lifestyle photo. A young Southeast Asian shopper at night holding a smartphone, face softly lit by the screen glow, pausing with a thoughtful, hesitant expression, thumb hovering. HEADLINE: Render this text exactly, spelled correctly, nothing else, on two lines:
"You Got Their Attention."
"What Happens Next?"
Typography: bold modern geometric sans-serif, white, placed in the darker empty area of the upper or lower third, the second line slightly highlighted in electric blue.

LOGO: If I attached the Feezless logo, place it small in the top-left corner exactly as provided. Do not redraw, recolor or alter it. If no logo is attached, leave the corner empty. Never invent a logo. No full-frame logo graphics.

BRAND STYLE: Premium fintech campaign. Colors: deep black #0E0E0E, clean white #FDFDFD, electric blue gradient accent (cyan to royal blue). High-end editorial quality.

After the image, reply with this caption exactly as written, ready to post:
```

## 1. 画像

**画像上のテキスト：** You Got Their Attention. What Happens Next?

**画像の方向性：** 感情に訴え、考えさせるシチュエーション写真。初日のようなフレーム全体のロゴグラフィックは使わない

```text Prompt ภาพ
Moody lifestyle scene: a young shopper at night holding a smartphone, face softly lit by the screen glow, pausing with a thoughtful, hesitant expression, thumb hovering. Shallow depth of field, dark ambient room with subtle blue highlights. Emotional and thought-provoking storytelling photo. The phone screen is blurred and unreadable. No full-frame logo graphics. Premium fintech brand campaign visual, color palette deep black #0E0E0E, clean white #FDFDFD and an electric blue gradient accent (cyan to royal blue). Leave empty space in the top-left corner for a logo and space for a headline. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```
