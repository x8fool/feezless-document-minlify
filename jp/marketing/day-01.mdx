## ワンクリック Prompt

ブロック全体をコピーして ChatGPT に貼り付け、Feezless のロゴファイルも添付してください。テキスト入りの画像とキャプションを一度にまとめて作成できます。

```text Prompt 1 คลิก · Day 01
Create one social media post image for the Feezless brand campaign "10 Days, 10 Different Stories" (Day 01 · Brand Reveal). Goal: make people remember the name Feezless.

FORMAT: 4:5 portrait, 1080x1350 px, for Facebook and Instagram feed.

SCENE: Minimal brand reveal poster. A single abstract blue gradient light streak sweeping across a deep black background, like a new horizon opening, with a soft glow and fine light particles. Elegant, confident, cinematic, very minimal.

HEADLINE: Render this text exactly, spelled correctly, nothing else: "A New Perspective on Payments."
Typography: bold modern geometric sans-serif, white, large, centered in the middle of the frame, generous letter spacing.

LOGO: If I attached the Feezless logo, place it centered above the headline exactly as provided. Do not redraw, recolor or alter it. If no logo is attached, leave that space empty. Never invent a logo.

BRAND STYLE: Premium fintech campaign. Colors: deep black #0E0E0E, clean white #FDFDFD, electric blue gradient accent (cyan to royal blue). Soft studio lighting, high-end editorial quality.

DO NOT: add any other text, screenshots, devices, product details, watermarks, bank or card brand marks.

After the image, reply with this caption exactly as written, ready to post:

โลกดิจิทัลเปลี่ยนวิธีที่ผู้คนซื้อขายกันไปแล้ว

Feezless คือแบรนด์ด้าน Payment Technology ที่มองการชำระเงินเป็นส่วนหนึ่งของโลกธุรกิจที่เชื่อมต่อกัน

นี่คือจุดเริ่มต้นของเรา

One Link. A Million Ways to Pay.

ติดตามการเดินทางของ Feezless

#Feezless #Fintech #PaymentTechnology
```

## 1. 画像

**画像上のテキスト：** A New Perspective on Payments.

**画像の方向性：** Brand Reveal。オリジナルのロゴとタイポグラフィを主役にし、スクリーンショットやサービスの詳細を入れすぎない

```text Prompt ภาพ
Minimal brand reveal poster. A single abstract blue gradient light streak sweeping across a deep black background, like a new horizon opening, with a soft glow and fine light particles. Large centered empty area reserved for a logo and bold typography. Elegant, confident, cinematic, very minimal. No screenshots, no devices, no product details. Premium fintech brand campaign visual, color palette deep black #0E0E0E, clean white #FDFDFD and an electric blue gradient accent (cyan to royal blue). Soft studio lighting, high-end editorial quality. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```
